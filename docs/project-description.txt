CORVQ is an industry-adaptive AI investigation and governance platform. One shared engine analyzes synthetic banking transactions and e-commerce orders using domain-specific rules and relationship graphs. Gemini agents investigate risks and generate structured reports. ProofGuard independently checks atomic factual claims against source records, marking them verified, unsupported, or requiring review. Analysts can remove evidence and rerun counterfactual scenarios to see which findings change. Human reviewers record decisions with evidence traceability and audit history. The project includes Firebase identity integration, workspace roles, a Firestore storage adapter, and automated tests and security scans. Risk indicators and AI hypotheses remain distinct from verified facts. CORVQ does not execute account freezes or seller suspensions.
